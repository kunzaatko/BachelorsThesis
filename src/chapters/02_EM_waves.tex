\chapter{Nature of Light}
\labch{ch:light}

To understand the fundamental physical limits present in optical sensing, it is important to
understand the physical nature of light. 

% TODO: Fix spacing with \emph command <07-11-23> 
Light was perceived in many different ways throughout the history. 
In his work, Sir Isaac Newton laid the foundations of the \emph{corpuscular theory of light}\sidecite{newton1704}.
It states that light is composed of particles called \emph{corpuscles} travelling in a straight line with finite
    velocity.

The corpuscular theory replaced the previous conceptions brought forward by René Descartes, who described light as a 
    propagating pressure in a medium. Paradoxically this older idea comes closer to the physics that describe light in
    present day optics.
